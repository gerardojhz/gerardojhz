# Gerardo J. Hernández Zavala (Jerry)

Commercial leader in payments, employee benefits and fintech, based in Mexico City.
20 years growing revenue at Edenred, Principal and MetLife; today co-founder & CMO of
Grupo NKD and professor at UNAM.

I'm not an engineer. I build the tools my commercial work needs with Claude:
automations, scoring models and trackers that take research and reporting off the
team's plate, so people spend their time with customers.

## Projects

- **[opportunity-radar](https://github.com/gerardojhz/opportunity-radar)**: a scheduled
  agent sweeps sources every morning, scores each opportunity with weighted rules and
  deal-breakers, and hands me a ranked shortlist with a next step. Same engine for my
  career pipeline and for B2B prospecting.
- **[Bompy y los Amigos del Bosque](https://bompystories.com)**: a bilingual children's
  book brand I created and launched: illustrated stories for ages 3-7 in Spanish,
  English and bilingual editions, sold on Amazon (print and Kindle) and as printable
  PDFs, plus personalized books with the child's name. Stories, illustrations and
  website made with Claude and Higgsfield.
- **[Prueba DISC · Grupo NKD](https://gerardojhz.github.io/disc-nkd/)**: the DISC
  assessment we use in Grupo NKD's leadership workshops: 24 word groups, natural vs.
  adapted style charts and a profile card per style. One HTML file built with Claude;
  runs on Google Apps Script in workshops. ([code](https://github.com/gerardojhz/disc-nkd))
